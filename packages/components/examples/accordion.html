Questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your device. Your progress syncs the next time you are online.

How do I save my game?

The game saves at every checkpoint flag. You can also press Esc and choose Save.

Where do my coins go when I quit a level?

Coins you pick up are kept only when you reach the end of the level or a checkpoint.

Can I change the controls?

Yes: Settings, then Controls. Every action can take a key and a gamepad button.
