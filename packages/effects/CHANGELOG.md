# @jaatster/blip-effects

## 0.1.0

First release. The opt-in effects: the dithered world backdrop with parallax and time of day, transitions (dissolve, dither, mosaic), drop text, typewriter, glint, bursts and the CRT filter. Every canvas draws whole device pixels at any screen scale; the sky keeps its sun, moon, stars and clouds clear of the words named in `avoid`; the CRT is a dither of ink, never a wash; the caret, the glint and the CRT's flicker stop after a few beats; the world, the CRT and the glint stay off paper.
